RCSCAT

Academic Team

RCSCAT is part of RCSC. It officially started it's tenure in 2024 following the club's inauguration in 2015. The purpose of the academic team is to strengthen and help the Olympiad and academic preparation of the students. It is a free and open source-wire for students of class VI-XII.
